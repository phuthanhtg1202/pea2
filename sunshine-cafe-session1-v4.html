🧒 Customer
Yes, please! 😊
👩 Barista
Here you go! (hands pretend cup)
🧒 Customer
Thank you! 🙏
👩 Barista
You're welcome! Bye bye! 👋

Game Time!

🎉 Fun ways to practice English! 🎉

Today's Activities
🍹 Activity 1: Real Blender Juice Station!
Students make REAL fruit juice with the blender! Practice English while doing it! 🍹
  • Teacher sets up the "Fresh Juice Station" with real fruits: mango, banana, strawberry, watermelon + blender, cups, honey, ice.
  • Student is the "Barista". Other students are "Customers" and ORDER in English from the blender menu.
  • The Barista-student must say each step aloud while making the juice: "I add the mango… I add ice… I blend!"
  • When juice is ready, pour into cups and say: "Here is your fresh mango juice! Enjoy!"
  • Rotate so every student gets a turn as barista. The whole class gets to taste! 🎉

🗣️ Customer: "Can I have a mango and banana smoothie with honey, please?"
🗣️ Barista: "Sure! First, I add the mango… then the banana… I add honey… and ice… I blend! Here you go!"
🗣️ Customer: "Wow, it looks delicious! Thank you!"
🗣️ Barista: "Enjoy your tropical smoothie! That's 4 dollars please!"

🔮 Game 2: Mystery Order
One player thinks of a drink/food. Others ask Yes/No questions to guess!
  • One student is the "Mystery Customer" – pick a secret item from the menu.
  • Other students ask YES or NO questions to guess the item.
  • Whoever guesses correctly becomes the next Mystery Customer!
  • The teacher counts how many questions it takes – fewer = better!

🗣️ "Is it hot?" → "No!"
🗣️ "Is it sweet?" → "Yes!"
🗣️ "Is it a fresh blended juice?" → "Yes!"
🗣️ "Is it a mango juice?" → "YES! 🎉"

🤪 Game 3: Crazy Café!
The teacher gives fun, silly situations! Students must respond in English.
  • Teacher gives the "Barista" a secret challenge card.
  • The "Customer" tries to order normally but something goes WRONG!
  • Students must solve the problem using English.
  • Funniest/best solution gets a bonus sticker! ⭐

😂 Challenge 1: The blender is broken! "I'm sorry, we can't make fresh juice today!"
😂 Challenge 2: Customer orders a drink that doesn't exist! "A rainbow smoothie?!"
😂 Challenge 3: Barista gives the wrong fruit! Mango instead of watermelon!
😂 Challenge 4: Customer has only coins, no bills! How many dollars is it?

⚡ Game 4: Speed Order!
How fast can you order AND describe your smoothie? Race against the clock! ⏱️
  • Set a timer for 45 seconds.
  • Student must complete a FULL café conversation including a blended juice order in 45 seconds!
  • Must include: greeting, order (with fruit names), size, pay, and goodbye.
  • If they finish in time – STICKER! If not – try again!

✅ Must say: "Hi!" + drink/fruit name + size + "How much?" + "Here you go!" + "Enjoy!" + "Bye!"
🏆 Bonus point: If they describe the blending steps correctly!

🎁 Reward System
🌟 Mỗi câu đúng = 1 sticker
🌟🌟 Hoàn thành cả conversation = 2 stickers
🍹 Mô tả đúng các bước xay sinh tố = 1 bonus sticker
🏆 Star Barista of the Day = huy hiệu đặc biệt + ảnh trên bảng danh dự!

Teacher's Guide – English Club

Story, key words, conversation games & a live blender demo! ☕🍹

🇻🇳 Once upon a time, in Vietnam...

Over 150 years ago, in 1857, visitors from France brought a special gift: a small green coffee tree. They looked at the high, misty mountains and the rich, red soil of Vietnam and thought, "This is the perfect home for coffee!" They were right. Before long, thousands of coffee trees were growing across the hills, covered in white flowers that smelled like jasmine.

But there was a small problem. In those days, there were no refrigerators! Fresh milk would turn sour very quickly in the hot, tropical sun. The Vietnamese people were clever, though. They found a silver tin of thick, sweet condensed milk that stayed fresh even in the heat. They poured the gooey, white milk into the bottom of a glass and let the dark, strong coffee drip on top. When they stirred it together with ice, they created Cà Phê Sữa Đá—a drink that tastes like a coffee milkshake!

As the years went by, coffee became the heartbeat of Vietnam. It wasn't just a drink; it became a way to make friends. People didn't want to hide inside big, quiet buildings. Instead, they put tiny plastic stools out on the sidewalk. Everyone—grandparents, students, and workers—sat together on these little chairs, side-by-side.

They used a special metal tool called a Phin. It sits on top of the cup like a little silver hat. Instead of rushing, people learned to be patient. They would sit and talk, watching the coffee go drip... drip... drip... into their glass while hundreds of motorbikes zoomed past them like a busy river.

Today, Vietnam is famous all over the world for its coffee. From the "Egg Coffee" in the north that tastes like sweet yellow cake, to the icy drinks in the sunny south, coffee is how Vietnam says "hello." Even though it's a drink for grown-ups, every child in Vietnam knows the wonderful smell of roasted beans and chocolate that fills the air every morning!

🔑 Key Words for Our Café

☕ Coffee / Cà phê 🥛 Condensed milk / Sữa đặc 🧊 Ice / Đá 🍹 Blender / Máy xay ➕ Add / Thêm 🫙 Blend / Xay 🫗 Pour / Rót 💵 How much? / Bao nhiêu? 🙋 Can I have…? / Cho tôi… 👩‍🍳 Barista

Teacher tip: Before starting, hold up each keyword card (or point to the word on screen). Say the word twice, then have the whole class repeat. Add a simple gesture for each one (e.g., for “blend” – shake your fists; for “pour” – tilt your hand). This physical action locks the words into memory!

🎭 Main Section: Barista – Customer Conversations

Goal: Every student speaks English in a real‑life café setting. Teacher first models, then students practice in pairs, then whole group.

  • 👩‍🏫 Teacher – Student Demo (5 min): The teacher acts as a customer, and a confident student acts as barista. Use the menu on screen. The teacher asks: “Can I have a mango juice, please?” Student barista answers: “Small, medium or large?” → “Medium.” → “For here or to go?” → “For here.” → “That’s 4 dollars.” → (teacher gives fake money) → “Thank you! Enjoy!” Repeat with another student so everyone sees how easy it is.
  • 💬 Student – Student Practice (10 min): Put students in pairs. One is barista, one is customer. They use the Grade 2 or 3 scripts (or the “Choose Your Own Order” script). Let them swap roles after 3 minutes. The teacher walks around, listens, and gives high‑fives for any English attempt.
  • 🍹 Real Blender Demonstration – Teacher ONLY (10 min): Bring a real blender, fruits (mango, banana, strawberries), honey, ice, cups. Important: Only the teacher touches the blender for safety. Students give instructions in English. Example: Teacher says: “What should I add first?” Students shout: “Add the mango!” Teacher adds mango. “Add the banana!” “Add ice!” “Add honey!” “Now blend!” Teacher blends and says “VRRRRR!” Then pours into cups and hands to the students. This way students practice the sequence vocabulary while watching a fun, real demonstration.
  • 🏆 Full Role‑Play “Sunshine Café is Open!” (10 min): Rearrange the room into a café. Two students act as baristas, the rest are customers. Give each customer fake money ($1, $2, $5). Customers must order a drink (and maybe a snack), ask the price, pay, and say thank you. Baristas must ask size, for here/to go, and say “Enjoy your drink!” The teacher circulates and gives stickers for complete conversations.

🎉 Wrap‑Up & Fun Games (5‑10 min)

🔮 Mystery Order (whole class): One student thinks of a secret menu item. Others ask Yes/No questions: “Is it cold?” “Does it have mango?” until they guess. Great for reviewing vocabulary!
⚡ Speed Order Race: Set a 30‑second timer. A student must say a full order (greeting, drink name, size, payment, thank you) before the buzzer. If they succeed, the whole class cheers.
🍹 Name That Smoothie: After the blender demo, ask students to invent a name for the smoothie they just watched. “Sunshine Blast”, “Mango Tango”, “Honey Volcano”. Write the top three names on the board and let the class vote. The winner’s name is written on a cup and celebrated!

⭐ Star Barista Award: Give a sticker or a paper badge to the student who used the most English or helped a friend. End with the goodbye ritual: everyone stands, bows like a café team, and says together: “Thank you for visiting Sunshine Café! See you next time!” 👋
