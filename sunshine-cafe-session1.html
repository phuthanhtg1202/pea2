🌟 Teacher Tips
Lớp 1–2: Cho bé cầm script card khi nói. Lớp 3–4: Script úp xuống, chỉ nhìn khi cần. Lớp 5: Không nhìn script! Encourage every attempt, correct gently AFTER the exchange ends.

Game Time!

🎉 Fun ways to practice English! 🎉

Today's Games
🔮 Game 1: Mystery Order
One player thinks of a drink/food. Others ask Yes/No questions to guess!
  • One student is the "Mystery Customer" – pick a secret item from the menu.
  • Other students ask YES or NO questions to guess the item.
  • Whoever guesses correctly becomes the next Mystery Customer!
  • The teacher counts how many questions it takes – fewer = better!

🗣️ "Is it hot?" → "No!"
🗣️ "Is it sweet?" → "Yes!"
🗣️ "Is it a drink?" → "Yes!"
🗣️ "Is it a smoothie?" → "YES! 🎉"

🤪 Game 2: Crazy Café!
The teacher gives fun, silly situations! Students must respond in English.
  • Teacher gives the "Barista" a secret challenge card.
  • The "Customer" tries to order normally but something goes WRONG!
  • Students must solve the problem using English.
  • Funniest/best solution gets a bonus sticker! ⭐

😂 Challenge 1: Customer orders 10 cookies at once!
😂 Challenge 2: Barista gives the wrong size!
😂 Challenge 3: Customer has too many coins, not enough bills!
😂 Challenge 4: Barista runs out of cups! "Can I use a bowl?"

⚡ Game 3: Speed Order!
How fast can you order? Race against the clock! ⏱️
  • Set a timer for 30 seconds.
  • One student must complete a FULL café conversation in 30 seconds!
  • Must include: greeting, order, size, pay, and goodbye.
  • If they finish in time – STICKER! If not – try again!

✅ Must say: "Hi!" + item + size + "How much?" + "Here you go!" + "Thank you! Bye!"
🏆 Class record: Who can do it fastest AND correctly?

🎁 Reward System
🌟 Mỗi câu đúng = 1 sticker
🌟🌟 Hoàn thành cả conversation = 2 stickers
🏆 Star of the Day (nhiều sticker nhất) = huy hiệu đặc biệt + ảnh trên bảng danh dự!
